Kanban Workflow Control

Organize work into custom stages: To Do, In Progress, Review, and Done. Instant drag re-ordering and status transition hooks.

Subtask Completion Meters

Deconstruct epics into testable checklist steps. Visual progress meters calculate real-time completion percentages across your sprint.

Clean Full-Stack Architecture
